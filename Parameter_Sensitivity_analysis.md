## Parameter Sensitivity Experiment Results
This document presents the experimental results of a sensitivity analysis conducted on key parameters of the RecFTT method across different datasets and recommendation models. The analysis primarily investigates the impact of two types of parameter variations:

1. Fixed loss lambda, varying top latent quantity: For the Movielens dataset, lambda is fixed at 0.1, and for the Amazon Grocery dataset, lambda is fixed at 1. Building upon this, the study evaluates the influence of varying top latent quantities (25, 50, and 100) on recommendation performance.
2. Fixed top latent quantity at 100, varying loss lambda values: For both the Movielens and Amazon Grocery datasets, the top latent quantity is fixed at 100, and different loss lambda values (0.05, 0.1, 0.2, or 1, 5, 10, respectively) are tested for their impact on recommendation performance.
